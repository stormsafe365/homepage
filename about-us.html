Our Story

We bring nearly seven years of experience in the steel building industry. We’ve seen how it works from the inside, and we’ve also seen where the customer experience can fall short.

That’s what led us to start StormSafe.

We wanted to build a company where customers aren’t treated like another number on a sales sheet. Where you can actually reach the people you bought your building from. Where questions get answered, details don’t get overlooked, and someone stays involved throughout the project.

And yes, starting a business with your spouse means you get to work together all day and then go home together. So far, we’re still getting along.

How We Work

We Believe You Should Know
Who You’re Doing Business With.

A steel building is a major investment. We don’t think you should feel like you’re on your own once you place your order.

We’ve seen how larger dealers can handle so many customers that the process becomes impersonal. You make the purchase, and suddenly you’re being passed from one department to another, explaining your project to someone new every time you have a question.

That’s not how we wanted to do business.

The usual way

  • Passed from one department to another after you buy
  • Explaining your project to someone new every time
  • Wondering who to call

The StormSafe way

  • The owners, from the first conversation through delivery and installation
  • Help with measurements, or a look at your property
  • Involved on-site and during installations

We’re a family-owned, owner-operated company, and we stay involved from the first conversation through delivery and installation.

If you need help figuring out measurements, want us to take a look at your property, or simply have questions about your building, we’re here. We make the effort to be involved on-site and during installations because we believe that’s part of taking care of the customer.

And no, you won’t get transferred to a call center where you have to explain your entire project all over again.

You’ll get us.

Who We Are

Small Business. Personal Service.
Real Experience.

We’re proud to be a small business.

Being small means we can actually know our customers, pay attention to the details, and give each project the attention it deserves. We’re not trying to be the biggest steel building company in Florida. Frankly, that’s a lot of phone calls.

We’d rather be the company our customers remember for being straightforward, responsive, and genuinely involved.

Where we build

We’re locals, based in West Palm Beach, FL. We serve customers throughout Florida, excluding Miami-Dade and Broward Counties, and also work with customers throughout the East Coast.

What we build with

Our buildings are manufactured in the USA using 100% USA-made steel, and we’re committed to providing a quality structure backed by people who genuinely care about the experience that comes with it.
