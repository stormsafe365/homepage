Planning Your Size

Size It Around
What Goes Inside.

The right size starts with what you’re putting in it, not the size of your lot. List every vehicle, trailer, boat and machine you’ll park, plus the workbench, shelves and mower. Measure each one (width, length and height), then add room to open doors and walk around. That gives you your floor size and your wall height.

Room to open doors

Leave about 3′ between vehicles and walls so you can open doors and walk around. The planner’s “Find my size” adds it for you.

Height, not just floor

Boats on trailers, lifted trucks and RVs need taller walls and taller doors. Side-wall doors need walls at least 2′ taller than the door.

Plan for the workshop

Workbenches, shelves, a mower and the fridge add up. Most owners wish they’d gone one size bigger, so plan the extras now.

Check your lot & county

Leave room for setbacks and your concrete pad, and check any HOA height limit. Your county’s wind zone sets your frame and doors. See permitting →

03

Then the height

Wall height is your clearance at the walls, and it caps how tall your roll-up doors can be. Florida heat is another reason to go taller: more air above you means a cooler building.

Wall heightWhat it fitsTallest roll-up · end wallTallest roll-up · side wall
9′Cars, SUVs, mowers and storage9′7′
10′Full-size trucks, vans and storage racks10′8′
12′Lifted trucks, boats on trailers, a car lift12′10′
14′Class C RVs, T-top boats, tractors14′12′
16′Class A RVs, fifth-wheels, box trucks16′14′
18′–20′Equipment, commercial bays, loft spaceBy designBy design

A single end-wall door can be as tall as the wall (2+ end-wall doors, 1′ shorter); a side-wall door must be at least 2′ shorter than the wall. Leave about 6″ over your tallest vehicle. Your roof peak sits higher than your walls, so check any HOA height limit.

Roll-up doors

Sized to your tallest vehicle plus about 6″. Hi-Impact rated doors for coastal Florida counties.

Walk doors

A 36″ × 80″ walk-in door, so you’re not opening the big door every time.

Windows

Light and airflow. Hi-Impact windows are required in some coastal counties.

Wainscot

A contrasting band of panel along the bottom of the walls.

Insulation

Cuts heat and condensation, worth it for a Florida workshop.

Lean-tos

Covered space down a side for a mower, trailer or boat.
