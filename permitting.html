The Basics

How Permitting
Works.

Installed buildings

In hurricane-prone Florida counties, you’ll need engineered plans for a permit. Installed StormSafe buildings come with engineered drawings for your county’s wind zone, ready for permitting. We guide you through the process.

Your wind rating

If your county’s code wind speed is 150 mph or less, our Standard frame (150 mph) covers it. Over 150, you need High-Wind framing (170 mph). In our high-wind counties, we build to 170 mph as standard.

Impact-rated doors & windows

Coastal impact counties require Hi-Impact rated windows and walk doors. We spec them automatically there, so your build passes the first time.

South Florida

Miami-Dade and Broward are Florida’s High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, with their own product approval rules, and Monroe has its own requirements too. Call us before you plan a build in these counties.
